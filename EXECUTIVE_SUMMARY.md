## 🎓 Key Learnings

### What Worked Best
1. **Single Primary CTA:** Eliminated decision paralysis
2. **Price Anchoring:** Reduced price objection
3. **Trust Badges:** Increased credibility instantly
4. **Accessibility First:** Benefits all users, not just those with disabilities
5. **Micro-interactions:** Small details create premium feel

### Best Practices Applied
1. **Progressive Disclosure:** Tabbed services reduce cognitive load
2. **Visual Hierarchy:** Clear path from awareness to action
3. **Semantic HTML:** Better SEO and accessibility
4. **Mobile-First:** Responsive design from ground up
5. **Performance:** Minimal size increase for maximum impact
